Features

Everything for your
weekly bazar.

🛒

Smart Item Browser

Browse 200+ pre-loaded grocery items across 8 categories. Tap to add — no typing required. Adjust quantity, unit, and price before adding to your list.

Core feature
✅

Shopping Mode

A focused checklist view for the actual bazar. Tap items as you buy them. Checked items move to the bottom with a satisfying haptic.

🎉

Celebration on Completion

When you mark everything bought, confetti bursts across the screen. Small joy, big motivation.

🌿

Seasonal Products

Jhuri automatically highlights fruits and vegetables currently in season in Bangladesh — based on the current month.

📊

Monthly Summary

Track your shopping habits and spending patterns over time. Know exactly where your grocery budget goes.

📁

Smart Archive & Reuse

Every completed list is archived. Duplicate any past list in one tap — perfect for weekly routines. Prices carry over so you always have a reference.

Time saver
⏰

Shopping Reminders

Set a reminder per list. Get notified at your chosen time on bazar day — tap to open the list directly.

✏️

Custom Items & Categories

Add anything not in the database. Create your own items and even your own categories — saved permanently to your personal library.
